About

The Female Nervous System Is Not a Default.

The female nervous system is not a male nervous system with a different hormone profile. Cannabis and psychedelics interact with it differently — different receptor densities, different metabolic pathways, different psychiatric risk profiles at different life stages. Most of the guidance women receive ignores this.

WNH produces primary-source, sex-disaggregated research guides covering what the neuroscience actually says. No advocacy. No anecdote. Peer-reviewed evidence, translated into language that is usable in a clinical encounter or a personal decision.

The clinician gap is the highest-stakes entry point: providers are routinely asked questions about cannabis and psychedelics by patients who are already using these substances. Most have no primary-source training on female-specific neurological effects.
