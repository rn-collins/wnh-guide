Privacy

Effective August 16, 2026.

The navigator runs in your browser. Questions, symptom timelines, medicines, substances, evidence notes, safety selections, and visit packets are not submitted to RN Collins, an AI model, or a database by this site.

Do not enter names, dates of birth, addresses, medical-record numbers, clinician names, employers, exact locations, credentials, images, or other identifying information. Use generalized descriptions.

You control exported Markdown, CSV, JSON, and print/PDF files. Clearing browser site data or using reset removes the locally saved copy.

Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary technical request information for delivery, security, and operational logging. External links follow their own privacy practices.
