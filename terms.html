Use boundaries

Effective August 16, 2026.

This educational tool organizes user-entered observations and evidence for discussion with a qualified clinician. It does not diagnose, assess causation, estimate individualized risk, recommend cannabis, psychedelics, supplements, medication, testing, or treatment, or establish that an intervention is safe or effective.

Do not start, stop, combine, or change a prescription, substance, or treatment because of this tool. Pregnancy, postpartum, lactation, fertility treatment, severe mental-health symptoms, neurological events, possible interactions, and rapidly worsening symptoms require tailored professional review.

The urgent-symptom examples are not exhaustive. Call local emergency services or seek urgent care when symptoms may be life-threatening or emergent.

Sources can become outdated, may not fit an individual’s life stage or clinical context, and may not report sex-disaggregated results. Users remain responsible for retrieving current sources and obtaining appropriate clinical review.
